# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Families and groups planning a stay for up to 26 people in Pueblo Tapao, Quindío, near Parque del Café.

## Purpose

Help prospective guests understand the chalet and its location, check availability, estimate a stay, and contact the owners to request a booking.

## Positioning

A spacious chalet for groups, with easy access to Parque del Café and the surrounding Coffee Region.

## Operating Context

Visitors browse the landing page on desktop or mobile, review the property and nearby experiences, use the availability calendar and browser quote, then contact the owners to confirm availability and payment. A quote does not hold dates or charge a payment.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The website presents factual property details, photos, location, availability, and an estimated quote. Booking requests, reviews, payments, and calendar persistence are not live in the review version. Preserve the existing reservation flow and bilingual Spanish/English behavior. Do not invent testimonials, services, prices, or availability.

## Brand Commitments

Product name: Chalet Giraldo. The user wants visitors to associate the stay with rest, nature, spacious accommodation, and proximity to Parque del Café. Preserve the current visual identity.

## Evidence on Hand

Confirmed details: Pueblo Tapao, Quindío; capacity up to 26 people; 5 bedrooms and 6 bathrooms; Parque del Café approximately 5 minutes away. Existing property photography and website copy are available in `public/`.

## Product Principles

- Keep property and booking information factual.
- Make it easy to understand the stay and request a quote.
- Preserve the current identity and Spanish/English experience.
- Set clear expectations that requests and estimates do not guarantee a booking.
